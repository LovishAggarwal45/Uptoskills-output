Prototype Notice & Architecture Specifications: This system operates as a functional research prototype and demonstration platform for outdoor surveillance under adverse atmospheric conditions.

  • CSRNet Density Regression: CSRNet regresses continuous spatial density maps \(\mathcal{D}(x,y)\) where crowd head count is obtained via spatial integration \(\iint \mathcal{D}(x,y) dx dy\). It does not output discrete bounding boxes or detector confidence percentages.
  • Optical Visibility Index (\(Q_{\text{trans}}\)): Reports spatial transmission mean \(\frac{1}{HW}\sum t(x,y) \in [0.05, 1.0]\) directly from the PhysicsGuidedDehazeNet transmission branch, providing physical atmospheric attenuation clarity.
  • Degradation Classification: Adverse atmospheric conditions (Fog, Rain, Glare) are categorized deterministically using physics and computer vision heuristics (Dark Channel Prior Mie scattering, vertical Sobel streak gradient asymmetry \(\partial I/\partial x\), and specular luminance bloom), explicitly documented as an optical analysis module.
  • Risk Formulation: Evaluated via \(\text{Risk} = \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{Count}}{\text{Max Capacity}} \times \text{Multiplier}_{\text{Weather}}\right)\), escalating through Low, Medium, High, and Critical alert thresholds.
